Interview notes
Constraints
Every port is a disc centred on the side it names, sitting on the node's border box, with a 2.5px info border and an info fill — border and fill the same colour, so a port never reads as a hole. The aimed-at port grows and shows an fg-inverse glyph at 60% of its size. In-flow dots live inside the card, so they fade with it; the drag tip is a z-999 screen-space overlay that does not.
