Left: at rest the bounding box is pointer-transparent, so a click in the blank area drills through to whatever is underneath. Right: a JS hit test against the stored points tagged the stroke data-sketch-hover, which re-enables pointer events and adds a 2px info-light drop-shadow. The disc is the eraser at its screen-space radius.
Strokes are perfect-freehand outlines: size 4, thinning 0.4, smoothing 0.5, streamline 0.35, pressure simulated off. Colour is a palette token resolved at render time, with ink-black and accent-white passing through as literals. A selected sketch behaves like any node — its whole box is grabbable — so a multi-selection can be dragged without hunting for a hairline.
