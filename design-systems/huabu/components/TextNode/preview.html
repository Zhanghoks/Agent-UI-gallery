No surface of its own: transparent fill, transparent border, just type on the canvas. Padding node-padding-x 12 / node-padding-y 4, line-height fixed at 1.5, and the size measured to fill the box — same measurement the frame-resize cascade re-runs, so a text node re-fits when its frame changes. Family is the person's choice of default, serif, mono or hand. Placeholder Type... is measured too, so an empty node is sized to its placeholder rather than one giant line.
