Tabs are h-9 min-w-20 max-w-48 with gap-1.5 px-2.5 text-sm, a 14px type glyph and a truncating title. A transient tab — opened by a single click and replaced by the next one — is set in italic, the same convention editors use. The drag overlay is the tab with a 1px edge-default border and elevation-drag, so the thing being dragged is literally the thing, lifted. Glyph and title stay fg-subtle until group-hover.
